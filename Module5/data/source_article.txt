Quantum Computing Overview

Quantum computing uses qubits instead of classical bits. Unlike bits that are 0 or 1, qubits can exist in superposition. This allows quantum computers to solve certain problems exponentially faster.

Key concepts:
- Superposition: qubits exist in multiple states at once
- Entanglement: qubits correlate regardless of distance
- Interference: amplify correct answers, cancel wrong ones

Current challenges:
- Decoherence: qubits lose state quickly
- Error rates: quantum gates are noisy
- Temperature: need near absolute zero

Applications: drug discovery, cryptography, optimization.
